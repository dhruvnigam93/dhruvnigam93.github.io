Seven failure points — what happened vs. what to do

✗ ON TAPE

✓ DO INSTEAD
Assume-and-confirm from a memorized anchor table: "I'll assume ~500M DAU and a ~200ms budget — stop me if that's off." Assumptions beat questions only when the numbers are sane.

2 · Architecture before the attack model S1 · Aug 29
✗ ON TAPE
Built a spam detector for an SMS-pumping question — misread who gets robbed ("block at client level") even though it was in the prompt. Pattern-matched to the comfort zone: text embeddings, content moderation.
✓ DO INSTEAD
Unfamiliar domain → 60 seconds on three questions before any box: who profits? what do they control? what can't they fake? AIT: fraudster splits carrier termination revenue · controls source traffic · can't fake OTP conversion.
3 · Labels assumed instant, complete, honest S3 · Aug 29 + 30
✗ ON TAPE
"Let's say we get some delivery confirmation" — the entire label design, on a question where DLRs arrive 2s–6h late, are missing 15–20% of the time, and grey routes spoof them. Aug 29: 1M fraud labels assumed into existence.
✓ DO INSTEAD
Three questions for every label: How late? How complete? Can it lie? One S1 question — "how fast and honest are DLRs?" — flips Aug 30 to Lean Hire. Unspoofable backup: OTP conversion — a route can fake a receipt, not the user typing the code.
4 · The $ pillar never lands S4 · 4/4 sessions
✗ ON TAPE
Reward = delivered − 1.2×cost — a coefficient, not a dollar. Precision-vs-recall flip-flop never resolved by cost. A fraud question literally about money: zero $ figures in 45 minutes.
✓ DO INSTEAD
Monetize the confusion matrix: t* = CFP / (CFP + CFN). Decisions: argmax P×Vtier − cost. One β for OTP and marketing is a category error.

5 · Cold start — 0 for 4 S3/S4 · every session
✗ ON TAPE

The 20-second recipe, verbatim: prior from cohort stats → capped exploration on low-value traffic → graduation criteria (min volume + quality bar). Never expose OTP traffic to unproven routes.
6 · Gaming reflex fires late or never S4 · 0/4 unprompted
✗ ON TAPE
"There is no other way to game this" (Aug 18, min 21) — while 5-second clips auto-cross a 50%-completion label on autoplay. Aug 17: a gameable session-length objective went unexamined for 44 minutes.
✓ DO INSTEAD
The moment you name a metric or label, ask: who profits from gaming it, and what's the cheapest exploit? Then attach the guardrail (length-conditional thresholds; length-controlled RM eval) to label and metric.
