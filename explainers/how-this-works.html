How this works

Added 2026-08-18

This is your private-ish hub for interactive HTML explainers — notes you write once and read on your phone. Every page here is self-contained (its own styles and scripts), mobile-first, and interactive where that helps.

Where it lives: /explainers/ is unlisted — it isn't linked from the site nav and carries a noindex tag, so search engines skip it. It is not private, though: the repo and site are public. Keep genuinely sensitive stuff out.

Adding a new one

From any Claude session, in any directory, run the skill:

/publish-explainer — then point it at a file or describe what you want. It builds a mobile-friendly interactive page, names it, records the date, pushes it, and gives you the URL.

Why self-contained?

No external CSS/JS means a page loads instantly, works offline once cached, and never breaks because a CDN changed. Everything it needs is in the one file.
