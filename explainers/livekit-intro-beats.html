Honest gaps & landmines

Own these if probed (never volunteer overclaims):
  • Sim user was scripted, not LLM-driven/adaptive.
  • Turn handling in sims = fixed --turn-interval wait, not response detection.
  • Judge scored 1–10; their pass/maybe/fail + forced tool call is better engineering — saying so is a credibility move.
  • Runs were manual, not CI-gated.
Do not say:
  • "CTO/co-founder" — he leads the Agents framework; founders are Russ d'Sa (CEO) and David Zhao (CTO).
  • Funding/valuation numbers — unverified.
  • Anything claiming to know Turn Detector v1 training internals. Instead ask: "is the acoustic branch trained jointly with the semantic one, or staged?"
  • "Time to first bite" — it's TTFB / time-to-first-audio.
